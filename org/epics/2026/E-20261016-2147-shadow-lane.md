## Goal

A research variant (a commit, an LLM/strategy config, and the stage it starts
from) registered by a research experiment runs every day beside production on
the same as-of inputs, in its own sandbox with a paper book, and produces a
daily comparison with the champion (Kimi K3 today). The owner promotes or
drops it from that evidence. Owner session 2026-10-16.

## Stories

1. Variant record `product/versions/v<n>/version.md` + `shadow.md`: commit, config, entry stage (stance | analysis | trading), R- source, start date — idea
2. Variant runner: worktree at the commit, sandbox data root `shadow/<variant>/`, entry stage enforced (a diff touching an earlier stage's code is refused) — idea
3. Store keyed by (model, config hash): a config change starts a new store; checkpoints never reused across configs — idea
4. Stance provenance: every stance record carries model, config hash and commit (research audit gap) — idea
5. Completeness gate inside the lane: a variant day counts only when its chain covers the window and its inputs match what production used; otherwise a gap, never a bad score — idea
6. Paper book per variant through TradingManager, approval and EMS with PaperBroker — idea
7. Scheduling: on the Mac after the production chain (00:30–~05:00), at most 2 variants at once, production always first — idea
8. Daily comparison vs champion and vs the variant's own backtest (parity) — idea
9. Promotion: registry.yaml champion/challengers, promotion.md with the owner's D- decision; a promoted variant still passes release validation — idea
10. Migrate the existing DeepSeek benchmark store (`benchmark_deepseek`) into a registered variant or retire it — idea

## Open questions

- Comparison metrics and window before a promotion is considered (Research protocol).
- Whether analysis-stage variants are allowed in v1, given docling and workflow cost on the Mac.
