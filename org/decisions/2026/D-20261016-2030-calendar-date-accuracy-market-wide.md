## Decision

Only one stance company has Q2FY27 documents in the index, so a 50-stance-company sample cannot exist yet. The measurement of stored meeting date against published results date is taken market-wide, across both BSE and NSE (some companies list on one only). Stance companies are a subset.

## Why

A sample-size target approved against a population that does not exist yet fails by construction (QA's catch on the spike).
